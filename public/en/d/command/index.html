Dutch firm Intelic's AI-based Nexus C2 software links multi-vendor sensors and interceptor drones, now being rolled out with Ukrainian brigades

2026-10-08

Defense News, carrying a Reuters report, describes Amsterdam-based software company Intelic and its Nexus command-and-control system, which links radars, sensors, cameras and interceptor drones from different vendors. Intelic has a €30 million (about $33.5 million) contract with the Dutch Defence Ministry to upgrade air defences. In a test in late September at a disused Dutch airbase, Nexus used radar and AI to detect and verify a mock target, a reconnaissance drone confirmed it with image recognition, and an interceptor drone was launched. Russian MoD drafts decree to create rapid response units within the military police

2026-10-08

Russia's Ministry of Defence has proposed adding the term "rapid response unit" to the Charter of the Military Police of the Russian Armed Forces, according to a draft presidential decree published on the federal portal for draft legal acts, TASS reported on 8 October. According to Ukrainian defense outlet Defense Express, the United Kingdom is withdrawing its Challenger 2 tanks from Estonia and replacing the existing armored battlegroup with a new "Mobile Anti-Armour Force" (MAAF). The British contingent in the country would reportedly grow from roughly 800 to 1,200 personnel. The new force emphasizes mobility, survivability, and drone integration, and would be equipped with high-mobility gear, modern weapons, and advanced unmanned systems tailored to Estonia's operational environment. The restructuring is directly tied by the source to Ukrainian combat experience and to the NATO Hedgehog 2025 exercise held in May, which involved about 16,000 troops from twelve NATO countries plus Ukraine. During that exercise, Ukrainian drone operators — reportedly around ten personnel from the 412th Separate Unmanned Systems Brigade "Nemesis" — allegedly managed to "destroy" two notional adversary battalions in roughly half a day, mock-destroying 17 armored vehicles and conducting about 30 strikes on other targets. Defense Express frames this within a broader shift toward a more drone-centric British doctrine, describing an eventual unmanned force structure that would be roughly 80% drone-based across land, naval, and air domains. SINGLE SOURCE — this is a report from Defense Express, a Ukrainian outlet, apparently relying on unnamed or anonymous sources. There is no official confirmation from the UK Ministry of Defence of this restructuring, the personnel figures, or the stated rationale. The "two battalions destroyed in one day" claim from Hedgehog 2025 is likewise not independently verified and should be treated as an unconfirmed claim rather than established fact.

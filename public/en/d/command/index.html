After a Putin–Witkoff meeting in St Petersburg, the US, Russia and Ukraine discuss resuming trilateral peace talks in November while missile and drone strikes continue

2026-10-22

According to Meduza, US special envoy Steve Witkoff met Vladimir Putin in St Petersburg on 21 October 2026. Afterwards, Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov said Russia was ready to resume negotiations on “the main issue”, which he described as a security framework for Russia and Ukraine after the conflict. He said talks could begin in mid-November, immediately after the US congressional midterm elections. Ushakov also said the “new approaches” Witkoff brought contained “not all that much that was useful” for resolving the crisis. Asked later whether the war was nearing an end, he suggested that “things are heading that way.” On 22 October Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov named Abu Dhabi as a possible venue. He rebuffed a question about whether Russia would prepare documents for signing. President Zelensky confirmed late on 21 October that Russia supported a three-way meeting of the negotiating teams and that Washington wanted to hold it as soon as possible, “no later than early November” in his estimate. He said the talks’ main task would be to prepare a meeting of leaders. Kyiv would support any ceasefire, full or partial. Witkoff flew on to Oslo to brief allies and wrote that the trilateral talks would “continue at the technical level.” President Trump said an end to the war was “perhaps closer than ever” and that he had recently spoken to Putin himself. The diplomatic signals did not halt the fighting. Overnight, Russia struck Kyiv with ballistic missiles, killing one person and injuring more than 20. Zelensky said this showed no real change in Moscow’s attitude. Ukraine launched drones at several Russian regions, including Moscow and Moscow Oblast, where two people were injured in the Novaya Moskva district. No dates or agenda have been formally agreed, and Russia’s readiness to discuss substance beyond the “security framework” remains unclear.

Russia’s “Dnipro” group is attempting to storm Orikhiv. Airborne (VDV) forces committed from the reserve advanced south-west of the town; Ukraine reportedly may have pushed them out of Novopavlivka, without visual confirmation. Russia’s “East” group has resumed a broad-front offensive towards Orikhiv and the Orikhiv–Novomykolaivka supply road, infiltrating Omelnyk. All positional claims come from Meduza’s reading of published video and statements from both sides and could not be independently verified.

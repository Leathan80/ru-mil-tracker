Former Defense Minister reveals Ukraine tested a new indigenous ballistic missile

2026-07-18SINGLE SOURCE

SINGLE SOURCE — Ukrainian defense outlet, based on a statement from a former Ukrainian official. Former Defense Minister Mykhailo Fedorov, summarizing his tenure just after the cabinet was dismissed on 14 July, said Ukraine successfully tested a new domestically developed ballistic missile that same day. He did not name it or give technical specifications but said the project falls under the Ministry of Defense's responsibility — likely separate from Fire Point's FP-7/FP-9 missile projects. According to Fedorov, the original technical requirements were fundamentally revised during development, producing a substantially more accurate weapon while cutting production costs by roughly 30%. No details on range, payload, or intended role have been disclosed. SINGLE SOURCE caveat: the only source is the outgoing official's own statement, as reported by Defense Express; there is no independent military or technical confirmation of the test, the missile's identity, or the claimed performance improvements. The timing — the same day the cabinet was dismissed — may color the political framing of the announcement.

Investigation: Russia has been rebuilding Facility 1335 in the Urals, the bunker linked to the 'Dead Hand' (Perimeter) nuclear command system

2026-09-26

A joint investigation by the Danish outlet Danwatch, The Sunday Times and the Dossier Center reports that Russia's Ministry of Defence has been carrying out a large modernisation of Object 1335 (codename 'Grot') since 2018. According to the reporting it serves as a command post of the Strategic Missile Forces and is linked to Perimeter (Perimetr), the Soviet-era backup command system known in the West as 'Dead Hand'. Perimeter was built in the mid-1980s to make sure a retaliatory nuclear strike could still be launched if the leadership were killed or communications cut off. Militarnyi describes the complex as having at least 24 rooms, with the main and emergency exits about 2.5 km apart. The evidence consists of two parts. The first is Russian defence procurement records: the investigators searched about 2.5 million records from 2018 to 2024 and found thousands of tenders naming Object 1335. These cover more than 22,000 tonnes of reinforcing steel, about 56,500 tonnes of cement, 600 km of electrical cable, 182 ventilation units, 5 MW of refrigeration capacity (bought in 2019), 14,000 lamps, heavy machinery, rock-drilling equipment, blast-resistant equipment and filters against radioactive, chemical and biological contamination. The records also list Western-made components such as Rockwool insulation and Danfoss and Belimo fittings. The second is satellite imagery, which shows extensive earthworks at the mountain's entrances and work on about six new access points. A Danish construction professor, Per Goltermann, called the quantities an 'enormous' construction project. These records and images establish that there has been a large construction and hardening effort at Object 1335. The link to a revived and more automated Perimeter comes from interpretation and from Russian testimony. Retired Major General Viktor Khalin, who says he worked on developing Perimeter in 2005–2010, wrote in a Russian veterans' magazine in January 2026 that the system was reactivated in 2023 and can now work 'not only in an automated mode but also in a fully automatic mode'.

2026-10-09

On the evening of 9 October 2026, several aircraft of the Rossiya Special Flight Detachment, which carries Russia's leadership and senior officials, were unable to land in Moscow straight away because of the threat of a Ukrainian drone attack, Meduza reports. At least three were Il-96s, the type used by the president, returning from Turkmenistan, where Putin had attended a Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) summit. Estonian drone maker KrattWorks is developing a jet-powered Shahed-like aerial target drone so militaries can train against Russia's main long-range drone threat.

The company says it has spare capacity and is open to moving some production abroad if demand is large enough. The article sets this against the scale of the threat. A September 2026 report by the Institute for Science and International Security estimated that Russia launched about 5,166 Shahed-type drones, decoys included, against Ukraine in August 2026 alone. On 28 September, Ukraine's Office of the President put the overall drone shoot-down rate at 70%, but only 57% for jet-powered Shaheds, a figure it called too low. Dutch firm Intelic's AI-based Nexus C2 software links multi-vendor sensors and interceptor drones, now being rolled out with Ukrainian brigades

2026-10-08

Defense News, carrying a Reuters report, describes Amsterdam-based software company Intelic and its Nexus command-and-control system, which links radars, sensors, cameras and interceptor drones from different vendors. Intelic has a €30 million (about $33.5 million) contract with the Dutch Defence Ministry to upgrade air defences. In a test in late September at a disused Dutch airbase, Nexus used radar and AI to detect and verify a mock target, a reconnaissance drone confirmed it with image recognition, and an interceptor drone was launched. Intelic has an office in Kyiv and says Nexus is being rolled out on the Ukrainian battlefield, supplementing Ukraine's own battlefield management system Delta. According to the company, Ukrainian brigades often field drones from different vendors and Nexus lets them connect these in one system; an Intelic engineer called it the "connecting tissue" between drone systems. The report puts this in the context of Russian air attacks: it says Russia uses high-altitude, jet-propelled attack drones and, citing Ukrainian Prime Minister Sergii Koretskyi, that in the first three weeks of September Russia launched 2,100 such drones and 136 missiles at Ukraine, killing 288 people. The Pantsir combines short-range surface-to-air missiles, twin 30 mm cannons and its own radar.

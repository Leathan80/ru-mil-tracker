Fighters from Black Forest unit struck MiG-29 and Pantsir-S1 at Khalino airfield in the Kursk region

2026-07-22SINGLE SOURCE

Censor.NET (EN) reports that fighters from Ukraine's 'Black Forest' unit — identified elsewhere as the 15th Separate Artillery Reconnaissance Brigade 'Black Forest' of the Ukrainian Ground Forces — struck a Russian MiG-29 fighter jet and a Pantsir-S1 gun/missile air-defense system at the Khalino airfield in Kursk Oblast, roughly 7 km east of Kursk city. The airfield hosts the 14th Fighter Aviation Regiment of Russia's 105th Mixed Aviation Division. This is the same underlying strike covered separately in the verified stream via Ukraine's General Staff, Euromaidan Press, Ukrainska Pravda and Kyiv Independent, which reported the drone strike on the MiG-29 (estimated $25 million) and the Pantsir-S1 (estimated $20 million) without naming a specific unit. Censor.NET's report adds the named-unit claim, attributing the operation to the Black Forest brigade, a formation previously credited with using reconnaissance drones to guide strikes (including Storm Shadow missiles) and with destroying an entire Russian air-defense regiment in 2024. This unit attribution is a single-source claim reported by Censor.NET, a Ukrainian outlet, and has not been independently corroborated by Russian sources or a second independent outlet; it should be treated as an unverified Ukrainian claim pending further confirmation.

Sources: censor

Ukrainian Drones Destroy Warehouses Near Moscow Despite Dozens of Pantsir Systems and Three S-400s

2026-07-22SINGLE SOURCE

Ukrainian long-range attack drones reportedly struck a cluster of logistics and fuel facilities in Moscow Oblast around 20 July 2026, hitting Wildberries' largest sorting center in Koledino, the 'South Gate' (Yuzhni Vorota) industrial park near Metkino, and an oil depot in Lvovskoye, all located 20-30 km south of central Moscow near Podolsk. Defense Express frames this as a follow-on wave after an earlier drone strike destroyed a separate Wildberries warehouse in Elektrostal, pointing to repeated Ukrainian targeting of Russian e-commerce and logistics infrastructure that supports the war effort. Citing OSINT analyst Mark Krutov, the outlet reports that roughly two dozen Pantsir gun-missile systems are deployed around Podolsk alone, with five positioned in the immediate vicinity of the struck sites, alongside three S-300/S-400 battalions nearby. Across the wider Moscow region, Russia has reportedly massed around 90 Pantsir systems and about 25 S-300/S-400 battalions in total — one of the densest air-defense belts in the country. Despite this concentration, the drones reportedly flew more than 750 km, crossing additional air-defense positions strung along Moscow's A108 outer ring road before reaching and destroying their targets. This account originates solely from the Ukrainian outlet Defense Express and has not been independently verified by Russian or third-party sources; Russian officials have not confirmed the strikes or the extent of the damage, and the cited air-defense figures cannot be independently checked at this time.

Sources: defexpress

These figures come from Ukrainian officials and have not been independently verified. Rubio and Hegseth denied administration involvement, Vice President Vance hinted at coordination with Musk, and Senator Richard Blumenthal has asked the Pentagon who ordered the restrictions. Who ordered the restrictions, and their removal, has not been publicly established.
